About Nova Hue

Capture Your Prism. Nova Hue is a salon-management and learning concept designed to help beauty professionals organize their work, connect with clients, and grow their skills.

Our Mission

We want to make the day-to-day work of cosmetologists and salon teams simpler by bringing useful tools together in one welcoming space.

For Clients

Explore appointment booking, hairstyle inspiration, and a more connected salon experience.

For Stylists

Explore tools for color formulas, client records, product information, education, and inventory.

For Managers

Explore scheduling, reports, inventory, and business-management features.

Nova Hue is a developing student project. Some features are demonstrations rather than live salon services.
