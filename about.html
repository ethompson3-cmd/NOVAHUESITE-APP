About Nova Hue

Our Mission

Nova Hue was created to help cosmetologists and salon professionals simplify their daily responsibilities while continuing to grow creatively and professionally.

From appointment booking and client management to hair color formulation, inventory, education, and financial organization, Nova Hue brings essential tools together in one place.

Our goal is to make professional resources more accessible and convenient for stylists at every stage of their careers.
